Journée Cancer du pancréas

Background

I was invited by the Cancéropôle Est to deliver a talk during the Journée Cancer du Pancréas (Pancreatic Cancer Day). The event took place during one day on the 1st of December 2023 in Besançon, France. The webpage of the event is available using this link.

I presented a work made in collaboration with the CHU de Strasbourg (Strasbourg's hospital) under the the supervision of Dr Gerlinde LANG-AVÉROUS, MD. The talk was co-presented with Dr Arthur BÉNÉDIC, Medical Intern at the Department of Pathology of the CHU de Strasbourg. Together, we presented a workflow intended to evaluate the immune infiltrate the the tumor micro-environment of the pancreatic cancer. Our new appoach focused on cell quantification with the introduction of mathematical morphology. The slides used during the talk are available in the resources (in French).
