Master 1 - Travail d'Étude et de Recherche

Background

The Travail d'Étude et de Recherche (TER) is a study and research project taking place during the first year of computer science Master's program. I worked on this project under the supervision of Dr Benoît NAEGEL, Assistant Professor of computer science at the University of Strasbourg and Senior Researcher in the IMAGeS team at the ICube UMR 7357 laboratory.

My work mostly involved C++ programming. I developed a codebase allowing to manipulate the concept of component-graph, an extension of component-tree, a well known structure in mathematical morphology. The main feature is that the component-graph was intended to manipulate label images instead of straighforwardly using the original grayscale or colour image.
