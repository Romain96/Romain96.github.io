Research Projects

This page lists all research projects I was involved with (current and past).

Tumor micro-environment modeling using graphs and mathematical morphology (2024-*)

This is my ongoing research focus.
I mostly work on improving the algorithms for the MSCT computation. I also work on using the MSCT to efficiently model the tumor micro-environment (TME) in order to automate the extraction of objects of interest (mainly cells) and specific pattern from the Whole Slide Images (WSI).

Multi-scale hierarchical structures for histological image analysis (2020-*)

This project is in essence the research I carried out during my thesis.
The main contribution of my thesis is the definition of a new hierarchical, multi-scale morphological structure able to efficiently model an image as a complex graph with embedded features and called the Multi-Scale Component-Tree or MSCT for short. This structure is based on the well-known component-tree and this offer efficient algorithmic construction. For more informations, you may visit the various talks I gave at conferences, most notably at DGMM 2024 conference for the formal presentation of the MSCT as well as at the ISBI 2024 conference for more a applicative aspect (all talks are also available though the Talk page) and the thesis defense page where my thesis manuscript as well as the defense presentation are available. You may also visit the Publication page where all published research articles are listed.

Histological cell counting methods development (2019-2020)

A project I worked on during my stay at the Cancéropôle Est.
I developed a method to count cells in H&E stained Whole Slide Images (WSI) using mathematical morphology in collaboration with the CHU de Strasbourg and the ICube laboratory (later published in my thesis manuscript). The codebase was written in Python, and some of this work has been continued during my thesis.

Multivalued component-tree for image analysis (2019)

A project I worked on when I was a research intern for 6 months.
My work was two fold : acquire the required knowledge in mathematical morphology (component-trees, component-graphs and multivalued component-trees) and develop a Python codebase to implement a newly-proposed efficient algorithm designed to compute a multivalued component-tree from a colour image. For more informations, you may visit the dedicated talk page containing the internship report and the final presentation (also available though the Talk page).

Demonstrating tool for the dual angorithm (2018)

A project I worked on when I was a Master student.
My work mostly involved C++ programming. I cleaned and improved a previous codebase written in C++. The objective of these scripts was to offer a practical demonstration tool showcasing the dual algorithm in discrete geometry. The demonstration tool was intented to be used for the IPOL online journal. For more informations, you may visit the dedicated talk page containing the project presentation (also available though the Talk page).

Component-graph on label images (2018)

A project I worked on when I was a Master student.
My work mostly involved C++ programming. I developed a codebase allowing to manipulate the concept of component-graph, an extension of component-tree, a well known structure in mathematical morphology. The main feature is that the component-graph was intended to manipulate label images instead of straighforwardly using the original grayscale or colour image. For more informations, you may visit the dedicated talk page containing the project presentation (also available though the Talk page).
