Welcome to my personal webpage

I am currently a Contracted Associate Lecturer at the University of Strasbourg, France. My work is two fold.

I am a researcher in the Data Science and Knowledge team of the ICube UMR 7357 laboratory in Illkirch-Graffenstaden. My research focuses mostly on mathematical morphology, graph-based structures and deep learning for medical (histological) image processing. For an overview of my research projects, consider checking the Research page. The Talks page provides an overview of all research or mediation related talks that I have given with additional resources such as slide presentations, demo codes or reports. The Publication page details all my published research works with additional resources (papers, posters, slide presentations...).

I am also a computer science teacher at the computer science department of the IUT Robert Schuman in Illkirch-Graffenstaden. I mostly teach about algorithmics and programming (C, C#, Java and Python) as well as UML modeling. If you are one of my students, consider visiting the Teaching page. It contains various resources for each teaching unit such as exercices, algorithms, codes and even some projects from the previous years (all in French).
